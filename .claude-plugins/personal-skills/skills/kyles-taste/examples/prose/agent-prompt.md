# Prompting an agent

How Kyle talks to a coding agent. Short sample, but it's the register agents see most often —
useful for calibrating what a reply should match.

---

> What seeding is going on there? Is that like our user and org seeds or something else? Also,
> could we mount a volume to make the ddb container more durable?

---

## What to copy

- **Question first, no preamble.** No "I was wondering if you could help me understand".
- **A guess offered with the question.** "Is that like our user and org seeds or something
  else?" Kyle hands over his current mental model so the answer can correct it. Reply by
  confirming or correcting the guess directly — don't ignore it and explain from scratch.
- **"Also," stacks a second, unrelated ask** rather than splitting into another message. Answer
  both. Don't drop the second one.
- **"could we…" is a real question, not a softened instruction.** He wants the assessment, not
  compliance. Say if it's a bad idea.
- **Domain shorthand goes unexpanded.** "ddb", "seeds". Match it — don't expand his abbreviations
  back at him.

## What this implies for replies

Short. Answer the question asked, in the order asked. If the answer is "no", lead with no.
